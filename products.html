Product Suite

Three levels of autonomous operational command.

Start with cognitive monitoring, add physical hazard detection, then graduate to a complete closed-loop management engine.

TIER 1 - Base

Bluework

Cognitive and psychosocial monitoring for teams that need stress, voice tonality, fatigue, and burnout intelligence without workflow automation.

For early visibility into workforce wellbeing

 TIER 2 - Professional

Argos-5

Adds active physical hazard detection, PPE compliance, environmental monitoring, and real-time safety alerts for operational environments.

For teams requiring dual-layered protection

 TIER 3 - The Archon Protocol

ArchonX

Complete autonomous command with bespoke AI personas, predictive liability modeling, and closed-loop rerouting across people, tasks, and risk.

For enterprise and government operations
01

Bluework

Bluework gives operators an early warning layer for cognitive strain, burnout risk, and psychosocial hazards. It is built for teams that need to see workforce risk clearly before adding physical safety automation.

Core Signals

Audio analytics for stress detection, fatigue identification, voice tonality analysis, and cognitive load trends.

Best Fit

BPO teams, call centers, distributed support teams, and operations starting with mental health and retention monitoring.

Primary Outcomes

  • Earlier stress and fatigue intervention
  • Improved retention and quality assurance visibility
  • Reduced psychosocial risk exposure
02

Argos-5

Argos-5 combines psychosocial intelligence with real-time physical hazard monitoring. It is designed for operational teams that need both worker wellbeing and environmental safety coverage in one system.

Physical Oversight

Computer vision monitors PPE compliance, environmental risks, blocked pathways, and site-level hazard signals.

Active Alerts

Real-time alerts route safety exceptions to the right people before small misses become operational incidents.

Primary Outcomes

  • Dual-layered physical and psychosocial monitoring
  • Faster safety intervention and compliance visibility
  • Lower operational risk across complex environments
03

ArchonX

ArchonX is the full closed-loop management engine. It does not just detect problems; it reallocates work, routes tasks, enforces breaks, and leads with bespoke AI management personas trained to match each client's culture.

Closed-Loop Command

Automated task rerouting, incident response, capacity balancing, and predictive liability modeling.

Bespoke Personas

AI leadership personas trained on company culture, operational policies, tone, and institutional knowledge.

Primary Outcomes

  • Autonomous operational response without middle-management delay
  • Real-time task delegation and workflow recovery
  • Enterprise-grade oversight for high-liability environments
