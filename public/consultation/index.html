    Questions founders ask

    Will you judge my AI-generated code?

    No. Most new software is now written with AI assistance, including by professional engineers. An app built with AI isn't a bad app; it's an app that hasn't had a second pair of eyes yet.

    I can't read code. Will I understand the report?

    Yes. Findings are written for founders: what the issue is, what could go wrong in business terms, and what fixing it costs.

    Which tools and stacks do you work with?

    Apps built with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Lovable, Bolt, Replit or v0, deployed onto Vercel, Supabase, Firebase, Railway, Render, Cloudflare or AWS. If your stack is unusual, we tell you before you pay anything.

    Do you keep control of my app afterwards?

    No. Every account (domain, hosting, database) is created in your name, on your email, with your payment method. We're given access while we work and removed when we're done.

    What if my app isn't ready to launch?

    Then the review says so, plainly, with what it would take to get there. That's a lot cheaper than finding out from your customers.

    Are you only for Brisbane?

    We're Brisbane-based and happy to meet local founders in person. Everything else happens over screen-shares, so we work with founders across Australia, and anywhere our time zones overlap.
