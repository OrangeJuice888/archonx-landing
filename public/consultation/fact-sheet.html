ArchonX Company overview · Brisbane, Australia

Automation and compliance surveillance of your own records.

ArchonX is an Australian software company. We connect the software your business already runs, automate the routine work across it, and keep your own records under continuous check. People make the decisions. The aim is lower running costs and less rework, measured every month.

The operating layer

Most businesses already own the software they need. The cost is in the work between the systems: re-keying, chasing, and checks nobody has time to do. We map how work moves through your business, connect your job, accounting, calendar and CRM software, and build only what is missing. Nothing you run today is replaced. Every account, phone number and subscription stays in your name, with a one-page runbook, and data is kept in the Australian region wherever the provider offers one.

Automation

Software does the routine work. Calls are answered and enquiries followed up by assistants that say they are AI. Quotes and invoices are drafted, and bills go into Xero, MYOB or QuickBooks. Documents are read, filed and logged for a person to check. Nothing faces a customer until it has been tested on your real calls and documents.

Compliance surveillance of your own records

Your own records are checked continuously against the rules your business has to follow. You and your advisers set the rules and the schedule. We build the checks. Checks we can build include a licence that lapses next month, a job booked with no safe work method statement on file, a shift rostered too soon after the last one, and a follow-up text queued for someone who unsubscribed. Anything out of line goes to a named person with the evidence attached.

People decide

The system flags. A person decides. Money, health, legal and tenancy decisions go through a person, every AI assistant says it is an AI, and every check and action is logged.

Cost and efficiency, measured

The aim is lower running costs and less rework: fewer missed renewals, less re-keying, less chasing paper before an audit. We do not promise a number before we have measured your work. A monthly report shows what the system did and what it got wrong.

Engineers alongside your team
